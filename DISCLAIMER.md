## 2. Navigation safety

The GN30 is marine navigation equipment. A wrong position, date or time can put a vessel and the people on it in danger.

- The modified firmware has **not** been tested, certified or approved by the manufacturer, a classification society or any authority.
- It was tested by one person on **one** unit. Your unit may behave differently.
- **Never rely on a modified unit as your only means of navigation.** Always carry and use independent means (charts, a second GPS, visual and radar fixes).
- After the fix, check the date, time and position against a trusted source before every use until you are satisfied, and again after any cold start.
- The fix corrects dates only until **2040-12-08**. After that the unit shows wrong dates again.

## 3. Risk to your equipment

The procedure means opening the unit, soldering or probing its circuit board, and erasing and rewriting part of its flash memory.

- A mistake, a bad connection, a power cut or a fault in these tools **can leave the unit unable to start**. The tools include many safety checks and a recovery path, but none of that is guaranteed to work.
- Static electricity, a slipped probe or wrong wiring can damage the unit or your computer.
- Opening the unit may damage its seals and its water resistance.

Do not attempt this unless you are competent to work on electronics and accept that you may lose the unit.
